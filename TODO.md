## Consistent frame times (Phase 15; the measuring is done)

The goal: the 1 % low near the average on every machine, no caps, the quality the GPU can give. See CLAUDE.md, Performance, Consistent frame times, for the numbers and the method.

1. **Garbage, what is left** (CLAUDE.md, Performance, Consistent frame times, The garbage, Phase 15b and 15c). ~1.4 KB a frame on the laptop at 57 fps in a quiet scene. Per tick (~35 KB/s, whatever the frame rate): the practice worker's message (~6 objects: the event, the buffer, its view; a SharedArrayBuffer ring would need cross-origin isolation, COOP and COEP headers) (done: pmove boxes no double in its Maglev graphs, 102 → 0). Per frame: `frame` (~5 objects: `dynres.frame`'s gap, `pollPads`' elapsed, maybe more), `draw` (~6: `fx.update`'s time, `perspective`'s fov, `weaponView.draw`'s sim time), the radar's redraw (~6 at 30 a second: `setTransform` and the arcs of the frame), `viewanim` / `viewmodel` (5). Rarely-run paths in baseline code while they run (the balloons' `toScreen`, ~20 a frame while one shows). First-session deoptimizations: `step` once at the first freeze end (the flight phase's `this.phase`, though `primeAnimator` reaches it), `reloadPose` at the first reload of a kind priming missed, `draw` at the first plant: ~0.5-5 s of baseline code each, once a page.
2. **DOM writes, the rest** (done: `--roundbar-w` off `body`, the hurt arcs and the comic words as Web Animations, `#fx` contained). The kill feed builds a row with `innerHTML` per kill (rare); the gunfire line (`.pop.ticker`) writes its text each shot and four properties each new line; `cl_showfps` / `net_graph` 4/s each when on; the scoreboard's `JSON.stringify` while Tab is held. Check on a GPU-composited PC (this laptop's Edge composites no animation, see CLAUDE.md, Phase 15d) before going further.
3. **The radar.** A redraw is 0.11-0.26 ms, 30 a second (3 % of the frames at 1000 fps): `drawImage` of the level a quarter, the clip a tenth. Two layers (the level on a canvas the compositor turns by a CSS transform, the dots on an overlay) trade the `drawImage` for a style write per redraw (~0.05 ms here): not clearly better. Done: the dark disc, the clip and the rims are CSS on `#radar` (the canvas draws the map and the markers alone). Left: the `drawImage`, or the radar drawn in WebGL with the frame.
4. **The tick** (measured, see CLAUDE.md, Phase 15d; done: practice over shared memory, 0.31 → 0.19 ms a tick). Left: online play's `writer.write` per tick (unmeasured), the prediction's `runCommand` (~0.05 ms) and the snapshot decode in `poll`.
