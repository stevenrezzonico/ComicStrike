Problems

    Keys

    • Right click or long press: the menu. Double click a shape: its settings. Tap a yellow measure to type it.
    • Wheel or pinch zooms; right, middle or two-finger drag pans. Alt drags without snapping.
    • Ctrl+Z / Y undo, redo, Del delete, Ctrl+D duplicate, Ctrl+X / C / V cut, copy, paste, Ctrl+A select all.
    • 1-9 0 the first shapes, Esc back to Select.
    • R rotate 90°, M mirror, F drop to floor, Q E turn 15°.
    • Arrows move by the grid, PgUp PgDn up and down, [ ] grid, Home frame all, L layout.
    • Rest the mouse on a button for what it does and its keys.
